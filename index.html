Flagship case study

From mass spectra to molecule predictions.

A closer look at the CASMI26 predictor and the decisions behind it.

Problem
Mass-spectrometry data is complex, and identifying likely molecular structures requires a repeatable workflow.
My approach
I built a data pipeline that cleans Parquet spectra, engineers features and trains a model for prediction.
Tech used
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Parquet and Streamlit.
Challenges
Working with high-dimensional spectra, choosing useful features and making results understandable.
What I learned
Good data preparation and clear evaluation matter as much as the model itself.
Screenshots
Screenshot captured from the working CASMI26 application, showing the mass-spectra upload and prediction workflow.
