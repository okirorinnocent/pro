Make these 2 edits to your existing index.html. Nothing else changes.

1) In <head>, right after the line:
       <meta name="theme-color" content="#4338ca">
   paste:
       <link rel="manifest" href="manifest.webmanifest">
       <link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="icons/apple-touch-icon.png">
       <meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-capable" content="yes">
       <meta name="mobile-web-app-capable" content="yes">
       <meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-title" content="Innocent">
       <meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style" content="black-translucent">

2) Just before the closing </body> tag, paste:
       <script src="pwa.js"></script>

Also: if your phone has a notch, change the viewport line to
       <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover">
(optional)
